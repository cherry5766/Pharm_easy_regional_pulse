# Recommendation Memo

## Title
Guntur sales increased +122.19% from April to May 2026. [HIGH]

## Context
Guntur recorded the largest-magnitude flagged sales change across the two transitions in this export. [HIGH] The 8% threshold is an operational review rule, not a statistical-significance test. [LOW]

## Key Insight
Both order volume and average order value contributed to Guntur’s increase; three categories account for most of the additional sales. [MEDIUM] The export does not establish why demand or order mix changed. [LOW]

## Evidence
- Sales: April INR 62,442.27 → May INR 138,738.93; increase INR 76,296.66 (+122.19%). [HIGH]
- Distinct orders: 51 → 77 (+50.98%); average order value: INR 1,224.36 → INR 1,801.80 (+47.16%). [HIGH]
- Wellness & Nutrition contributed +INR 33,787.42, Medical Devices +INR 23,276.06, and Lab Tests +INR 11,012.02: together 89.22% of the increase. Orders in these categories rose 14 → 29. [HIGH]
- The five largest orders represented 38.17% of April sales and 27.46% of May sales. [HIGH] May sales were less concentrated in those orders, but this does not exclude large-order effects. [MEDIUM]
- Company sales rose 2.89%; excluding Guntur, sales fell 4.49%. [HIGH] Guntur’s increase contrasts with the rest of this export; wider market behaviour is unverified. [MEDIUM]
- June Guntur sales were INR 99,745.18: −28.11% versus May, +59.74% versus April. [HIGH] This partial reversal does not resolve source completeness or causality. [MEDIUM]

## Recommendation
Review all May Guntur orders, focusing on the three contributing categories, before changing targets, inventory or staffing. [MEDIUM] Ask the regional lead to reconcile source counts and document possible causes within five working days of the review. [MEDIUM]

## Next Check
Compare July sales with June’s INR 99,745.18 and April’s INR 62,442.27 after processing the July export. [HIGH] Persistence above June would support further investigation; a return near April would weaken the sustained-shift interpretation. [MEDIUM] Combine this evidence with source reconciliation before any structural decision. [MEDIUM]

## Assumptions
**Single unverified assumption:** monthly exports are complete and consistent; April’s low order count reflects actual orders rather than missing records. [MEDIUM] April’s Guntur count is 51. [HIGH]

Possible promotions, availability changes or onboarding are hypotheses requiring source confirmation, not established causes. [MEDIUM] Profit has 94 imputed values among 2,100 orders; the sales figures above do not depend on profit imputation. [HIGH]
